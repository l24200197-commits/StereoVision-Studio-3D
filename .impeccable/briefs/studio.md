# Surface brief: Studio (index.html)

Mode: Operate (Studio) with a Persuade-leaning portada.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Studio is an on-set camera reference monitor; the portada demonstrates convergence itself. Refuses the generic dark dashboard of nested rounded cards and neon glows.

OWN-WORLD: Warm graphite (#0e0e0d → #1d1d1b), pure-black monitor field, hairline rules, white corner framelines, OSD readouts in Martian Mono, labels in condensed Archivo caps, display in expanded Archivo 800. Red = left eye, cyan = right eye, peaking-yellow focus ring, white primary action. No glows, no glass.

STORY: Visitor sees the title converge from a red/cyan pair into white, understands "this lines up two views", picks a module from the input selector, and lands in the Studio where steps 1–4 (módulo, archivo, calibra, VR) are evident.

FIRST VIEWPORT: OSD bar top; black monitor frame (~55vh) with framelines, live disparity readout top-left, STBY tally + timecode; giant StereoVision title centered; below the frame a three-input selector bar (Anaglifo, Imagen SBS, Video SBS) as the primary action; author line at the bottom.
